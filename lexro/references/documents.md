# Drafting documents

## Principles

1. **The document describes reality, not a template.** Do not draft a privacy policy or terms without intake: role, products, data and money flows, third-party tools, targeted countries. A polished document that does not match practice creates risk instead of reducing it.
2. **Verify the acts before drafting.** Clauses that depend on deadlines, thresholds or wording required by law rest on the text verified live. Otherwise, mark them in the draft with `[TO VERIFY: ...]`.
3. **Do not copy other sites' documents.** Besides copyright issues, they describe other businesses.
4. **Clearly mark what the user must complete or decide**: `[COMPLETE: ...]`, `[DECISION: ...]`. Translate these markers into the document's language (in Romanian: `[DE VERIFICAT: ...]`, `[COMPLETEAZĂ: ...]`, `[DECIZIE: ...]`).
5. **Document language** is independent of the conversation language: documents for consumers in Romania are drafted in Romanian (verify the language requirements); other versions can be offered too. Use the official Romanian legal terminology.
6. **Versioning**: each document has an effective date and version number; keep an archive of previous versions.
7. **Human validation**: at the end, list the clauses that must be validated by a specialist.

## What you can draft and what must be validated

| Document | Full draft? | Mandatory validation for |
|---|---|---|
| Terms and conditions (B2C) | yes, after intake | liability limits, applicable law and jurisdiction, withdrawal exceptions, subscriptions, unilateral changes |
| Seller terms (marketplace) | yes, after intake | liability between parties, suspension and termination, ranking, payments, P2B, DSA |
| Privacy policy | yes, after the data map | international transfers, legitimate interest bases, special categories, profiling |
| Cookie policy | yes, after the technical inventory | match with what actually runs on the site |
| Withdrawal form | yes | conformity with the legal model in force |
| Returns policy | yes | exceptions, costs, deadlines |
| DPA (processor agreement) | yes, based on the GDPR minimum clauses | liability, limitations, audits, sub-processors, transfers |
| Joint controller arrangement | draft | allocation of responsibilities |
| Record of processing activities | yes, from the data map | completeness |
| Breach response procedure | yes | actual roles and deadlines |
| Data subject request procedure | yes | verified deadlines |
| Legitimate interest assessment | draft | the conclusion |
| DPIA | structure and draft | the conclusion; usually requires a DPO |
| Notice-and-action mechanism (DSA) | yes | conformity with DSA requirements |
| Accessibility statement | draft | conformity with national requirements |
| B2B contracts (services, licence, IP assignment) | draft | everything, before signing |

Do not draft as final text: responses to authorities, breach notifications to the authority, court documents, tax opinions. You may prepare drafts or structures, clearly marked for validation.

## Minimum structures to check

### B2C terms and conditions
trader identification; definitions; contract formation (steps, moment); prices and payment; delivery; right of withdrawal (or exception) and procedure; legal guarantee of conformity and, separately, commercial guarantee; liability (without unfair exclusions); complaints, ADR and ANPC (no ODR link if the platform no longer exists); personal data (reference to the policy); changes to the terms; applicable law (without depriving consumers of mandatory protections); version and date.

### Privacy policy
controller and contact details (DPO, if any); for each purpose: the data, the basis, the duration; recipients and categories of recipients; transfers and safeguards; rights and how to exercise them; the right to complain to ANSPDCP; the source of data not obtained from the person; automated decisions and profiling; whether providing data is mandatory; version and date.

### Cookie policy
what they are and which technologies are used; table: name, provider, purpose, duration, type; how consent is given and withdrawn; reference to the privacy policy; update date.

### DPA
subject matter, duration, nature and purpose; types of data and categories of data subjects; obligations and rights of the controller; the minimum clauses of GDPR Art. 28 (documented instructions, confidentiality, security, sub-processors, assistance, deletion or return, audits); transfers; annex with technical and organisational measures; list of sub-processors.

## Always include at the end
- the list of verified sources and the verification date;
- the list of remaining `[TO VERIFY]`, `[COMPLETE]`, `[DECISION]` fields;
- clauses requiring specialist validation and the type of specialist;
- a recommendation to update when processes, providers or legislation change.
