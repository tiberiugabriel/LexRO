# Escalation: when to refer to a specialist, and to whom

Escalation does not mean refusing help. Offer what you can (explanations, immediate steps, questions to prepare, documents to gather) and say clearly what must be decided by a specialist and why.

## 1. Urgent situations (running deadline or immediate risk)

Handle these before full intake.

### Data breach or security incident
Immediate steps to tell the user:
1. contain the incident (revoke access, change compromised passwords and keys, isolate affected systems), without destroying evidence;
2. note the moment you became aware of the incident: the GDPR notification deadline depends on it (72 hours where the breach poses a risk; verify the text);
3. document what happened: what data, how many people, which systems, what was done;
4. assess the risk to individuals (sensitive data, financial data, passwords, volume);
5. check other obligations: NIS2, CRA, customer contracts, payment processor, insurance;
6. contact the DPO or a specialized lawyer immediately and, if needed, an incident response specialist.

Do not write the final notification to the authority without validation; you may prepare a draft following the authority's required structure.

### Inspection, notice or request from an authority (ANPC, ANSPDCP, ANAF, ANCOM, DNSC, Competition Council, BNR)
1. note the response deadline in the document;
2. do not send responses without consulting a lawyer (or a tax advisor, for ANAF);
3. gather the requested documents and history;
4. contact the specialist immediately.

### Formal complaint, demand letter, lawsuit
Lawyer, immediately. Procedural deadlines are running.

### Possibly dangerous product, consumer accident
Product compliance specialist + lawyer. Check notification and recall obligations (GPSR, sectoral legislation).

### Fraud, mass payment disputes, compromised customer accounts
Payment processor, security specialist, lawyer; may also be a personal data breach.

## 2. Thresholds by domain

Escalate (RED) when any of the following occurs.

| Domain | Situation | Specialist |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer | disputes, ANPC inspection, repeated complaints, significant cross-border sales | lawyer |
| Platforms | establishing the marketplace liability structure; DSA notices | lawyer |
| Products | sector-regulated products with uncertain classification; imports without documentation; recalls | product compliance specialist; customs advisor |
| GDPR | special categories of data; large-scale profiling; systematic monitoring; DPIA; complex transfers; breaches | DPO or specialized lawyer |
| Cookies/marketing | complaints, inspections, large-scale campaigns | DPO or lawyer |
| Tax | any registration, option or correction decision; activity in several states; audits | tax advisor or chartered accountant |
| Payments | the business holds or redistributes other people's money; BNPL; crypto; wallets | lawyer specialized in financial services |
| Security | uncertain NIS2 classification; products with digital elements (CRA); incidents | security specialist; lawyer |
| AI | possibly high risk or prohibited practice; automated decisions with significant effects | specialized lawyer; DPO |
| IP/competition | infringement notices; exclusivity or parity clauses with stakes | specialized lawyer |
| General | large sums at stake; conflict between official sources; contradictory authority practice; untransposed or incorrectly transposed directive | lawyer |

## 3. Preparing the file for the specialist

Offer to prepare a short file, which reduces the cost of the consultation:
1. **the situation** in 5–10 lines (role, what is sold, to whom, where, money and data flows);
2. **the precise question** for the specialist;
3. **established facts** and **missing facts**;
4. **identified acts** and their verification status (with official links);
5. **options** the user sees, with identified risks;
6. **documents to bring**: contracts, current terms and policies, screenshots of the checkout and cookie banner, the provider list, correspondence with the authority;
7. **running deadlines**.

The format is in response-format.md.

## 4. What not to do when escalating
- do not just say "consult a specialist" without saying which one and why;
- do not refuse to explain the general rules;
- do not give a firm conclusion and then "cover" it with a recommendation to see a specialist; if the situation is RED, the conclusion is "this cannot be determined without a specialist", and the explanation is why.
