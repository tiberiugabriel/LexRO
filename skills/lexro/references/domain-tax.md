# Domain: tax (VAT, invoicing, e-Factura, OSS/IOSS, DAC7)

Starting acts: acts-registry.md, section E. All verified live.

## Special rule for this domain

Romanian taxation changes frequently, sometimes several times a year, including rates, thresholds, deadlines and forms. Therefore:
- **do not quote from memory** any rate, threshold, cap or deadline; take them only from the verified official source, with the version date;
- the default confidence level is more conservative than in other domains: a tax conclusion without live verification is RED;
- for any decision affecting returns or payments, recommend confirmation by a tax advisor or chartered accountant.

When storing or presenting a threshold, use the structure: value / applicable from / applicable until / source / verified on.

## What to establish

### 1. Tax status
- legal form and tax regime (PFA, SRL as microenterprise or under profit tax, etc.); check the current rules, which change frequently;
- VAT registration and the small enterprise exemption threshold (check the current value and the cross-border regime of the exemption, amended at EU level);
- VAT rates applicable to the goods or services (verify; they were changed recently, according to the compiler).

### 2. Place of supply
- goods sold at a distance to consumers in other EU states: the common EU threshold above which VAT is due in the destination state (verify the value); the OSS option;
- electronically supplied, telecommunications and broadcasting services to consumers: VAT in the customer's state (with the common threshold); OSS;
- intra-EU B2B: reverse charge, VIES validation of the VAT number, recapitulative statements;
- services to customers outside the EU: place-of-supply rules;
- imports: import VAT, **IOSS** for consignments under the value threshold (verify the threshold).

### 3. Marketplaces and the deemed supplier
Check whether the platform is deemed, for VAT purposes, to have bought and resold the goods, in particular for:
- low-value imported consignments;
- supplies within the EU by sellers not established in the EU.

If so, the platform has its own VAT and record-keeping obligations. Also check record-keeping obligations for platforms facilitating supplies even when they are not deemed suppliers.

### 4. Invoicing and e-Factura
- the obligation to issue invoices and their content;
- **RO e-Factura**: check which transactions it is mandatory for (B2B, B2G, B2C) on the analysis date, transmission deadlines and the rules for individuals without a communicated CNP; the regime has been extended and changed in stages;
- invoicing of platform commissions;
- the regime for fiscal receipts and cash registers for online sales (check whether and when it applies).

### 5. Special situations
- vouchers (single-purpose or multi-purpose) and gift cards;
- account credit, loyalty points;
- refunds, chargebacks, subsequent discounts: adjusting the taxable amount;
- subscriptions, advance payments;
- digital services to consumers outside the EU.

### 6. DAC7 (platform reporting)
- check whether the user is a "reporting platform operator" (relevant activities: sale of goods, personal services, rental of immovable property and of means of transport);
- seller data to collect, due diligence procedures, reporting deadline to ANAF, exclusions (e.g. below a certain number of transactions or value; verify);
- clearly distinguish: the **platform's** reporting obligation ≠ the **seller's** tax obligations.

### 7. Permanent establishment and activity in other states
If there is staff, warehouses or a fixed activity in another state, the question of a permanent establishment and registration there arises. Escalate.

## Common pitfalls
- using thresholds or rates from memory;
- confusing the small enterprise VAT exemption with exemptions for exports or intra-EU supplies;
- distance sales above the EU threshold declared in Romania;
- digital services to EU consumers treated as B2B without proof of the customer's taxable-person status;
- incorrect VAT treatment of vouchers;
- a marketplace ignoring DAC7 or deemed supplier rules;
- assuming e-Factura does not apply to B2C.

## Specific escalation
- any decision on registration, options (OSS/IOSS) or correcting returns: tax advisor or chartered accountant;
- ANAF inspection, assessment decisions, audits: tax advisor + lawyer;
- multi-state structures or permanent establishment: international tax advisor.
