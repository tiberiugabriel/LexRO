# Domain: platforms, marketplaces, intermediaries

Starting acts: acts-registry.md, section B. All verified live. Article references are indicative: confirm the numbering in the official text.

## When it applies

When the user stores or intermediates third-party content, products or services: marketplace, service or booking platform, platform with user-generated content, provider directory, classifieds platform.

## Step 1: determine the DSA category

The DSA has tiered obligations. First determine the service's category:
1. intermediary service (mere conduit, caching, hosting);
2. hosting service;
3. online platform (hosting + dissemination to the public at the user's request);
4. online platform allowing consumers to conclude distance contracts with traders (marketplace);
5. very large online platform (VLOP), only by Commission designation.

Check the exemptions for micro and small enterprises (applying to some platform sections, not to all DSA obligations) and their exact conditions.

## Step 2: DSA obligations to check

All intermediaries:
- single point of contact for authorities and for users;
- EU legal representative for providers outside the EU;
- terms and conditions describing content moderation;
- transparency reporting (check exemptions).

Hosting services:
- notice-and-action mechanism for illegal content (Art. 16);
- statement of reasons for restriction decisions (Art. 17);
- notifying authorities of suspected serious criminal offences threatening life or safety (Art. 18).

Online platforms:
- internal complaint-handling system (Art. 20) and information on out-of-court dispute settlement (Art. 21);
- trusted flaggers (Art. 22);
- measures against misuse (Art. 23);
- prohibition of deceptive design (dark patterns) (Art. 25);
- advertising transparency (Art. 26);
- recommender system transparency (Art. 27);
- protection of minors (Art. 28).

Marketplaces (platforms allowing distance contracts with traders):
- **traceability of traders** (Art. 30): what information you collect, how you verify it, what you do if it is false;
- compliance by design (Art. 31): the interface allows traders to display mandatory information;
- informing buyers about illegal products or services (Art. 32).

Enforcement in Romania: the competent authority (Digital Services Coordinator) and sanctions in the national act. Verify the act (acts-registry.md, B).

## Step 3: P2B (relationship with sellers or providers)

If the platform intermediates for business users, check:
- terms and conditions for business users: clarity, notice of changes;
- statement of reasons for restriction, suspension or termination;
- transparency of ranking parameters, including the influence of payments;
- differentiated treatment (e.g. when the platform also sells its own products);
- data access;
- internal complaint system and mediation (check the small enterprise exemption).

## Step 4: who is liable to the consumer

The central question: is the platform an intermediary or the seller?
- Who appears as seller at checkout, on the invoice and in communications?
- If an average consumer may believe the platform is selling, the platform's liability exemption may not apply (verify the DSA text on marketplace liability towards consumers).
- Informing the buyer, before the order, whether the seller is a trader (consumer law obligation introduced by Omnibus).

## Step 5: related domains triggered

- **GPSR**: marketplaces have their own product safety obligations (single point of contact, cooperation with authorities, interface for safety information, removal of dangerous offers). See domain-products-import.md.
- **DAC7**: reporting sellers to the tax authority. See domain-tax.md.
- **VAT**: deemed supplier in certain situations. See domain-tax.md.
- **Payments**: if the platform collects and redistributes money. See domain-payments.md.
- **GDPR**: platform – seller roles (separate controllers, joint controllers or processor) and what data the seller sees. See domain-gdpr.md.
- **Competition**: parity, exclusivity, self-preferencing clauses, exchange of sensitive information. See domain-ip-competition-advertising.md.

## Minors

If the platform is accessible to minors, check: protection measures (DSA), the prohibition of profiling-based advertising to minors (check the conditions), the age of digital consent in Romania (Legea 190/2018 or the act in force; do not quote the age from memory), Commission guidance on the protection of minors.

## Common pitfalls
- considering yourself "just an intermediary" when the platform controls price, payment and the buying experience;
- not collecting and verifying seller data;
- no real mechanism for reporting illegal content;
- not explaining ranking parameters or paid placements;
- assuming the small enterprise exemption removes all DSA obligations.

## Specific escalation
- marketplace liability structure (seller – platform – buyer contracts): lawyer;
- collecting money for third parties: lawyer specialized in financial services;
- notices from the Digital Services Coordinator or authorities: lawyer, immediately.
