# Acts registry (starting map)

## How to use this registry

This is the **starting point** for research, not the source of the answer. It was compiled by an AI model without live, act-by-act verification. Each act must be verified on the official source before being used in a conclusion (protocol in sources-and-verification.md).

The list is **not exhaustive**. Also look for acts not listed here: implementing rules, authority orders, sectoral legislation, new acts adopted after compilation.

**The "Confidence" column** only describes how sure the compiler was about the act's identifier, not whether the act is in force:
- **H** (high): the identifier is very likely correct.
- **M** (medium): the identifier or details must be verified as a priority.
- **ID?**: the act exists or very likely exists, but its identifier must be found (usually via "national transposition measures" on EUR-Lex or via Portalul Legislativ).

Romanian act types: **Legea** = Law; **OUG** = Government Emergency Ordinance; **OG** = Government Ordinance; **HG** = Government Decision. Search Romanian acts by type, number and year (e.g. "OUG 34/2014").

Compilation date: October 2026. Anything that happened afterwards (and some things that happened before) may be missing.
