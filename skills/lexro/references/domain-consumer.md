# Domain: consumers and e-commerce

Starting acts: acts-registry.md, section A (and I for accessibility). All verified live.

## When it applies

When goods or services are sold to consumers (natural persons acting outside their profession). For pure B2B, most protections below do not apply, but general contract law does, and the information obligations under e-commerce legislation may also apply in B2B (verify).

## What to check

### 1. Mandatory information on the site
- trader identity, contact details, registration details, tax ID;
- information required by e-commerce legislation (information society services);
- information in Romanian for consumers in Romania (verify the exact requirements).

### 2. Pre-contractual information (distance contracts)
- main characteristics, total price including taxes, delivery costs, payment and delivery arrangements;
- contract duration, termination conditions, auto-renewal;
- existence and conditions of the right of withdrawal, or why it does not exist;
- legal guarantee of conformity and, separately, any commercial guarantee;
- digital content information: functionality, compatibility, interoperability;
- additional information for marketplaces (whether the seller is a trader, how obligations are shared; introduced by Omnibus).

### 3. Order process
- the order button clearly indicates an obligation to pay (verify the required wording);
- confirmation of the contract on a durable medium;
- proof of accepted terms and their version.

### 4. Right of withdrawal
- the period and when it starts (different for goods, services, digital content); verify the exact text;
- the model withdrawal form and how withdrawal can be exercised;
- the **online withdrawal function** introduced at EU level by amending Directive 2011/83/EU; verify the application date and Romanian transposition;
- the exceptions (personalized goods, perishable goods, goods sealed for hygiene reasons, digital content supplied with express consent, etc.) and the exact conditions for relying on them;
- services started during the withdrawal period: express consent requirements and consequences;
- refund deadline and who bears the return cost;
- incomplete information on withdrawal: check the effect on the withdrawal period.

### 5. Conformity and guarantees
- legal guarantee of conformity for goods (OUG 140/2021 or the act in force) and for digital content/services (OUG 141/2021 or the act in force);
- the obligation to update digital content;
- commercial guarantee: separate from the legal one, cannot restrict it;
- right to repair and liability for defective products (recent EU acts; verify transposition and application).

### 6. Prices, discounts, commercial practices
- total price displayed correctly, unit price where applicable;
- **prior price when announcing discounts** (rule introduced by Omnibus): verify the reference period and exceptions in the Romanian act;
- prices personalized through automated decision-making: consumer information;
- fake urgency, fake stock levels, untrue "X people are viewing now", hiding costs until the end, subscriptions that are hard to cancel: misleading or aggressive practices;
- dark patterns (also check DSA for platforms);
- environmental claims ("eco", "green", "climate neutral"): rules in Directive 2024/825 and its transposition.

### 7. Reviews
- information on how the trader verifies that reviews come from actual buyers;
- prohibition of fake reviews, bought reviews and showing only positive reviews without disclosure (check the blacklist of practices).

### 8. Subscriptions and auto-renewal
- clear information on duration, renewal, price after the trial;
- the cancellation method (must not be harder than subscribing; verify the exact requirements);
- transition from free trial to paid.

### 9. Terms and conditions
- potentially unfair clauses: broad liability limitations, unilateral changes without reason, waiver of rights, jurisdiction favouring the trader, disproportionate penalties;
- clear and accessible language;
- versioning and archiving.

### 10. Cross-border sales
- if you actively target consumers in other states, the mandatory protections of the consumer's state may apply regardless of the chosen law (verify the applicable private international law rules);
- geo-blocking: you cannot refuse or redirect consumers from other EU states based on nationality or residence, subject to the regulation's exceptions;
- language, currency, delivery.

### 11. Dispute resolution
- information about ADR (alternative dispute resolution) and ANPC; verify the wording required in Romania;
- **ODR: negative verification is mandatory.** The European ODR platform has been closed, according to the compiler. Verify its status before recommending any ODR link or wording. If the user's site still has the link, recommend updating it.

### 12. Accessibility (European Accessibility Act)
- check whether the user's service falls within scope (e-commerce services are included; verify the definition);
- check the exemption for microenterprises providing services and its criteria;
- technical requirements (usually via harmonised standards; verify the current reference standard);
- the accessibility statement or information required;
- competent authority and sanctions under the Romanian transposing act.

## Common pitfalls
- copying another shop's terms: obligations depend on product, role and processes;
- stating "no returns accepted" for products where withdrawal applies;
- confusing the legal guarantee with the commercial guarantee;
- calculating a discount from a temporarily inflated price;
- believing B2B excludes all obligations although orders come from private individuals;
- keeping a link to the ODR platform;
- assuming an English-language site delivering across the EU does not target consumers in other states.

## Specific escalation
- disputes or ANPC inspection, repeated complaints: lawyer;
- contested subscription terms or collective actions: lawyer;
- significant sales in other states with different laws: lawyer with cross-border practice.
